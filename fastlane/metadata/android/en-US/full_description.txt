LinkLift is a native Android application built with Jetpack Compose and Material 3 that lets you analyze, preview, and download high-resolution videos, audio tracks, and images from supported web links and social media platforms.

Powered by an embedded Python runtime via Chaquopy, LinkLift leverages yt-dlp and Instaloader on-device combined with native MediaMuxer stream merging for combining separate high-definition video and audio streams seamlessly.

Key Features:
- Multi-Platform Support: Extract media from YouTube, Instagram (posts, reels, carousels), TikTok, X/Twitter, Reddit, Pinterest, SoundCloud, Vimeo, and other sites supported by yt-dlp.
- Stream Merging (MediaMuxer): Downloads separate video and audio streams in a foreground service and multiplexes them locally into standard MP4/WebM files.
- In-App Fullscreen Player: Built-in media player with gesture controls, playback speed controls, and video quality selection.
- Batch & Playlist Downloads: Expand playlists and channels, preview individual entries, and queue bulk downloads.
- System Share Sheet Integration: Share any media link directly from your browser or social media apps into LinkLift.
- 100% Tracker-Free: Zero Google Play Services dependencies, zero analytics SDKs, and complete privacy.
